## Open the counter

In Busog, press Log, then Counter (Turo-turo in the Philippines region). Tap a dish tile to select it; tap again to
remove it. Selected dishes show a check and an outline.

## Set rice and portion

Choose half, regular or extra for the selected dishes. Choose your rice cups or no rice. The displayed total
recalculates from the ingredient snapshot. Open the small detail arrow to inspect the dish math.

## Confirm and correct

Choose the meal and press Log this plate. The diary shows each dish and rice as a separate entry with its source. Tap
any entry to change its serving or remove it.
