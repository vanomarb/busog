## Start with assumptions

Open You and edit the adult profile. The suggested target uses Mifflin–St Jeor resting energy, an activity multiplier
and the chosen 250 kcal adjustment for a deficit or surplus.

## Use or replace the suggestion

Press Use suggested target to copy the calculated number into your editable daily target. Protein and carbohydrate
percentages are energy shares; fat receives the remaining share.

## Save and revisit

Save goals updates the diary summary. This is an educational formula, not a measured need or clinical plan. Your diary
stays intact, so you can compare how different assumptions change the display.
