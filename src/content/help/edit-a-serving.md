## Open the entry

Tap a food row in Today. Food detail shows its current gram weight and nutrition. Press Portion to open the serving
picker above the detail sheet.

## Choose a source serving or grams

Use a listed USDA portion where one exists, or enter grams. The serving chooser is a separate nested layer. Use this
portion returns to the review without moving the sheet below.

## Save the change

Press Save portion. The diary, meal subtotal, summary and chart all use the same updated entry. Browser Back or a
downward drag closes the top sheet one layer at a time.
