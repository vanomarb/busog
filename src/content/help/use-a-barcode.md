## Use Search and scan

Open Log, then Search and scan, then Barcode. Camera scanning is available where the browser supports the native
detector and camera access; manual digits always work.

## Check the result

Enter 8–14 digits and press Look up barcode. A complete Open Food Facts record opens for portion review. Confirm the
product identity and compare its label before adding it.

## Handle a missing result

A missing, offline or incomplete product does not get invented nutrition. Check the digits, try later, or use USDA
search for an appropriate ingredient. Open Food Facts attribution remains with logged records.
