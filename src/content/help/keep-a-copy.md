## Export your changes

Open You and choose Export JSON. The browser downloads your diary, goals, weights, water and source credits. The
optional personal USDA key is excluded.

## Understand local storage

Edits are stored only in this browser when storage is allowed. Private browsing, storage limits and clearing site data
can remove them. Export gives you a portable record; Busog has no cloud sync.

## Restore the starting point

Choose Start fresh, then Restore the sample diary. This brings back the sample week and the default profile. Export
first if you want to keep edits.
