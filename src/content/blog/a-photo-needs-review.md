## A photo starts a conversation

Pictures can make food entry feel approachable. They do not tell the full story of cooking oil, ingredients hidden in
sauce or the weight of a serving. The review step is where you decide what belongs in your diary.

## What the preview does

Busog does not run a recognition model. Its Snap flow offers three licensed sample images and a short staged reading
sequence. Each sample maps to the ingredients of one of Busog’s compositions. The simulated recognition label stays
visible throughout.

## Check the mapping

The next screen lists ingredients with their USDA IDs and gram amounts. You can change each amount, including setting an
unwanted item to zero. Confirming adds the reviewed items with a Snap source label. Nothing is uploaded, and the
sequence makes no accuracy promise.

## Why show the limitation

A photo is a starting point, not a verdict. A clear review step and visible source records matter more than a confident
guess, so Busog makes you the last word on every gram.
