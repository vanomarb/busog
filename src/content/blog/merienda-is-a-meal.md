## Name the moment naturally

Merienda is already a useful name for an afternoon eating occasion. Busog uses it beside breakfast, lunch and dinner
rather than hiding it in an awkward extra category. The diary should feel like a place for your day, not someone else’s
schedule.

## A small plate still has components

A banana, boiled egg and sweet potato can be separate entries or an explicit recipe composition. Either way, their
nutrition comes from the same USDA ingredient records and measured edible weights. A familiar name does not replace the
source information.

## Change the portion, keep the context

The serving picker lets you choose a listed USDA serving or enter grams. A photo sample is another possible starting
point, but its staged recognition still needs your review. Every route reaches the same diary and carries its source
label.

## Make room for ordinary days

You can add water, edit a food and return later without creating an account. This concept explores how a nutrition tool
could fit around everyday eating. It does not promise a particular outcome or prescribe what your merienda should be.
