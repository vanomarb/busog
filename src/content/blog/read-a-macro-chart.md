## Know what the colors represent

Busog labels protein, carbohydrate and fat wherever it uses color. Each macro total comes from scaled food records. The
chart shows those totals against the editable gram targets derived from your chosen energy split.

## Percent of which goal

The circles in Macro balance show the proportion of the calorie target recorded on each day. The lines below describe
each macro as a proportion of its own gram target. Future days use hatching, so an unrecorded day does not look like
measured zero intake.

## Averages are context

Progress calculates averages over the selected 7, 30 or 90 days. A partially logged day is still partial data. The
demonstration includes mock history so the chart is useful to explore, but that history is fictional and does not show
real outcomes.

## A record is not a grade

A number above or below a target is information about the record and its assumptions. It is not a moral judgment. You
can edit the goal, correct an entry or simply return to the next meal. The interface should make those choices easy.
