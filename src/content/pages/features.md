## One diary, three entrances.

Point at dishes in Turo-turo, review a staged sample photo, or find a USDA ingredient and look up a packaged food. Each
confirmed food stores its gram weight, source and meal. Tap a diary row to revisit the serving.

## See the source behind the serving.

A curated shelf of 110 everyday USDA foods supports local search. Each keeps its original description and FDC ID.
Twenty-five original recipes, Filipino and international, calculate totals from those ingredients. How we counted this
shows the work.

## Notice the pattern.

Today includes meal subtotals, a calorie ring, labelled macro bars, water and a streak derived from the diary. Progress
shows 7, 30 and 90 days, a goal line, macro averages and dated weight logs you can edit.

## Make the frame fit.

The same app URL chooses a phone or web layout. Phone sheets follow a downward drag and nest above each other. Desktop
gives the diary a sidebar, centered dialogs and a sticky summary. Both have light and dark themes.

## Keep the demonstration yours.

Change the goals, export JSON or restore a fresh mock diary. No account is required. Storage failure leaves the app
usable for the visit. Installed and cached assets support the local diary and bundled food search offline.
