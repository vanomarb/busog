## One diary, three entrances.

Point at dishes in Turo-turo, log from a photo, or find a USDA ingredient and scan a packaged food. Each entry keeps its
gram weight, source and meal. Tap any row to change the serving.

## See the source behind the serving.

A curated shelf of 110 everyday USDA foods searches instantly, even offline. Each keeps its original description and FDC
ID. Twenty-five recipes, Filipino and international, calculate their totals from those ingredients, and "How we counted
this" shows the work.

## Notice the pattern.

Today brings your calories, macros, meals, water and streak together. Progress shows 7, 30 and 90 days against your
goal, average macros and a weight trend you can edit.

## At home on any screen.

Busog picks a phone or desktop layout on its own. On phones, sheets follow your finger and stack; on desktop you get a
sidebar, dialogs and a summary that stays in view. Light and dark themes included.

## Yours, on your device.

Set your goals, export your data as JSON, or start fresh. No account required. Install Busog to your home screen and the
diary and food search keep working offline.
